# Rulings — simulation architecture

The live list of OPEN rulings: what the agent assumed to keep moving, and
what waits for the user's decision. Newest first; PREPEND new entries.

HOW AN ENTRY IS WRITTEN (the user, 2026-10-12): one entry is ONE decision
in a few lines — what is assumed now, and what must be ruled. It is not a
diary. What was built and how it was checked goes to CHANGES.md. What the
work found — measurements, defects, surprises — goes to FINDINGS.md.

An answered ruling moves to Resolved as one or two lines with its answer;
the law it creates goes into PHILOSOPHY.md or the code in the same change
set. Nothing here is design until ruled. Stamps are Yerevan time, the
moment the entry was first written.
